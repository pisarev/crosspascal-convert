Model derivative notice

All model parameter, tokenizer and graph artifacts listed in model.json
are supplied as a derivative of the source identified in SOURCE-model.json.
They retain the model terms in LICENSE-model.txt and USE-POLICY-model.txt.

Huihui-ai describes its checkpoint as an abliterated derivative of Google's
Gemma 3n E4B instruction model. Bartowski converted that checkpoint to the
mixed-format Q8 GGUF identified by the source hash and pinned revision.

CrossPascal generates native TVM text-decoder graphs and file-backed
parameters from that GGUF. The CPU profile decodes its quantized weights
to FP32. The CUDA profile copies the original Q8_0 signed integers and each 32-value
block scale, widening the source FP16 scales exactly to FP32. Auxiliary
BF16/F32 matrices expand to FP32. The graph uses FP32 arithmetic and omits
unused FP32 projection copies. The selected
backend, enabled modalities and hashes are recorded in model.json.

When the optional pinned projector is supplied, CrossPascal also generates
a native TVM vision graph from the Gemma 3n image encoder and projection.
Visual parameters expand to FP32. Missing media markers are appended to
the tokenizer without renumbering the original vocabulary. Text-decoder
weights retain the conversion described above. The selected modalities
and context are recorded in model.json.
